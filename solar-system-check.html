What Solectrics would compare

1 — The design

Whether the proposed solar array, inverter architecture and battery strategy actually fit your household — not just whether the equipment looks good on paper.

2 — The trade-offs

What you gain or give up by choosing the cheaper, middle or higher-spec option — including future battery cost, backup capability, export limits and expansion.

3 — The whole-life value

Whether the system still makes sense once you factor in future EVs, hot water, heating, warranties, monitoring and how much solar you’re likely to use directly.
