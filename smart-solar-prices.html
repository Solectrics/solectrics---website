Est. $200 installed

Hot Water Heat Pump

Efficient hot-water heating; many models have their own scheduling controls.

Smart EV Charging

Est. $1,500–$2,500 supplied & installed

Schedule charging for suitable solar-production or lower-cost electricity periods.

Washing Machines and Other Household Loads

$0 extra equipment cost

Use built-in delay-start timers on suitable washing machines, dishwashers and dryers.

Pricing guidance: EECA's solar calculator assumes $200 for a hot-water timer and $1,000 for a diverter; EECA estimates around $7,000 for a hot-water heat pump and $1,500–$2,500 for a smart EV charger, supplied and installed. Using existing appliance timers generally needs no new equipment. These are indicative NZ benchmarks; actual installations may vary and Solectrics quotes separately. Sources: solar calculator, hot-water heat pumps, EV charging and smart-energy controls. Read how these options work in our Smart Home Energy guide.

About these prices: Prices shown are indicative Waiheke Island installed prices (including GST) based on benchmark configurations submitted by SolarDarity, a collaboration between Solectrics and Solarity. Larger high-energy homes are individually quoted. Prices are not fixed offers for every property. Standard assessment, network application administration, inspection, compliance certificates, handover and ordinary Waiheke freight/travel are allowed for. External retailer/metering charges are excluded; scaffolding, unusual roof or access conditions, additional cable runs, switchboard changes and specialised backup work may alter the final quote. Panel and battery suitability, essential-load or whole-home backup capability, and Vector connection/export requirements are confirmed through site assessment and final design. We provide a tailored written quotation before installation. See EECA's general solar cost guidance.
The Solectrics approach

You may not need to buy everything.

We look for the smartest place to spend your money first — not simply the biggest system we can sell you.

01 — USE IT

Use solar directly

Run suitable high-load appliances while your panels are producing.

02 — CONTROL IT

Make timing automatic

Timers, smart hot-water controls and solar-aware devices can put more daytime solar to work.

03 — STORE IT

Add storage when it helps

If you still have useful surplus — or you want backup — add a battery sized around your actual needs.

Not ready for a battery? No problem.

We can design your solar system battery-ready from day one, so you can start with solar and smarter energy use now and add storage later.
