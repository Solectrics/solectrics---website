The big opportunity

HOT WATER HEAT PUMP

EECA says water heating makes up around one-third of household energy bills, making it one of the most worthwhile places to look for savings.

Solectrics can source and coordinate the installation of a hot-water heat pump suited to your household, your hot-water use and your property.

A hot-water heat pump gathers warmth from the surrounding air and transfers it into your water instead of creating all the heat directly from electricity.

We help select the right type and size, plan how it will work with your solar, and coordinate the electrical and plumbing installation.

Solar-powered comfort

HEAT PUMP + smart controls

An efficient heat pump can warm your home while your solar panels are producing energy.

Your home can carry some of that warmth into the evening, reducing the grid electricity needed after solar production fades. Timers and smart controls help the heat pump run during the most useful solar hours while maintaining a comfortable temperature.
