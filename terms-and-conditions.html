Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to Solectrics solar, battery, smart-energy and general electrical quotations. The scope, price and payment stages in the customer’s quotation form part of the agreement.

1. Acceptance and contract

The contract is formed when the customer accepts the quotation in writing and pays any required deposit. A quotation is valid until its stated expiry date and may be revised afterwards if supplier prices or availability change.

2. Scope of work

The work is limited to the equipment and services stated in the quotation. Solar and battery designs remain subject to site verification, usable roof area, electrical design, network requirements and final technical approval. Solectrics will discuss any material design change before proceeding.

3. Price and GST

Prices include GST unless the quotation expressly says otherwise. The price assumes the site conditions and access described in the quotation. Excluded work is not included unless it is specifically listed or later approved as a variation.

4. Deposit and payment

The deposit covers equipment, materials, freight and supplier commitments and may be more than 50% of the total price. Solectrics is not required to order equipment or confirm an installation date until the deposit has cleared. Stage and final payments are due at the milestones shown in the quotation.

5. Variations and unforeseen work

Customer-requested changes and work arising from concealed or unforeseen site conditions will be priced separately. Solectrics will seek approval before carrying out additional chargeable work, except where immediate action is reasonably necessary for electrical safety or to protect property.

6. Customer responsibilities

The customer must provide safe and reasonable access, disclose known hazards or defects, keep work areas clear, provide accurate property and electricity information, and obtain the owner’s consent if the customer is not the property owner. Reliable internet access may be required for online equipment monitoring.

7. Inspection, network, retailer and meter charges

The quotation will state whether independent inspection and normal network or export-application administration are included. Separate retailer, meter-provider, network or additional inspection charges are excluded unless specifically allowed for. Third-party approval timeframes, tariffs, export rates and meter changes cannot be guaranteed.

8. Timing and delays

Installation dates are estimates until confirmed. Solectrics is not responsible for reasonable delays caused by unsafe weather, supplier shortages, freight disruption, third-party approvals, illness, unforeseen site conditions or events outside its reasonable control. Solectrics will keep the customer informed.

9. Existing roof, wiring and equipment

Solectrics will take reasonable care but is not responsible for pre-existing defects, deterioration, non-compliant work or concealed damage that could not reasonably be identified beforehand. If an issue affects safe completion, Solectrics will explain the available options and any additional cost.

10. Solar performance and savings

Where the quotation includes solar or smart-energy equipment, production, self-consumption and savings depend on weather, shading, electricity use, equipment settings, tariffs and load timing. Any forecast is a good-faith estimate and is not a guarantee of a particular bill, generation figure, saving or financial return.

11. Battery and backup

Where a battery or backup system is included, backup is limited by available charge, inverter output, selected backup circuits and equipment settings. Loads beyond the available output may cause backup to stop. Manufacturer instructions and protected settings must be followed.

12. Warranties and maintenance

Manufacturer warranties apply according to their published terms. Solectrics will perform installation services with reasonable care and skill and assist with appropriate warranty administration. Warranty support does not cover misuse, unauthorised alterations, external network events, internet failure, storm damage, accidental damage or manufacturer exclusions.

13. Equipment availability and substitutions

If quoted equipment becomes unavailable or compliance requirements change before installation, Solectrics will discuss any proposed substitute and price effect. No material substitution will be made without the customer’s agreement.

14. Access, postponement and overdue payment

If work cannot proceed because safe access or required preparation has not been provided, Solectrics may charge reasonable attendance or rescheduling costs after explaining them. Solectrics may pause further non-safety work while an undisputed invoice remains overdue and may recover reasonable collection costs actually incurred.

15. Cancellation

If the customer cancels after acceptance, Solectrics may retain or charge reasonable costs already incurred, including non-refundable supplier charges, specially ordered equipment, design work and completed labour. Any remaining deposit balance will be refunded. Statutory cancellation rights are not affected.

16. Concerns and disputes

Please raise concerns promptly so Solectrics can inspect the work and try to resolve them fairly. If agreement cannot be reached, either party may use an appropriate independent dispute-resolution service or the Disputes Tribunal.

17. Consumer rights

Nothing in a quotation or these terms limits or excludes any right or remedy under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, Fair Trading Act 1986 or any other law that cannot legally be excluded.
